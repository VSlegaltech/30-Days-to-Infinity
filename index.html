Create a dedicated subreddit such as r/30DaystoInfinity, then point these links to it.

Best default: Reddit

Use Reddit for public, searchable Q&A, chapter-specific threads, and author AMA sessions. It is the best fit when readers need threaded answers that can help future readers too.

Use LinkedIn for reach

LinkedIn is better for author posts, launch announcements, professional credibility, and essays that bring people back to the book site.

Use Meta for private groups

Facebook Groups work well for warmer peer support, but answers are less searchable and the experience depends heavily on moderation.
